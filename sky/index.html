How it works. The date is the seed. It picks the time of day, the land, the weather, the one small thing in the scene and the title, so every day gets a sky no one has seen before and the same day always gets the same sky. The moon is that evening's real moon. The song is composed from the same seed and played by a synthesizer in your browser. Come back tomorrow for the next one.

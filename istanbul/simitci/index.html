The Simit Seller

Morning in Balat. The simit seller calls, a basket comes down on a rope, and the cats get their share.

Download MP4 All episodes

How it's made. Nothing here is a photo, a drawing file or a video model. A JavaScript function paints each frame from nothing: the painted houses of Balat with their bay windows, the seller and his tray, the teyze and her basket, the cats. The oud-like melody is in makam Rast, with a darbuka keeping time.

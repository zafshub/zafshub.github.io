The Ferry

Sunset over the old city. A ferry crosses the Bosphorus, and the gulls follow it for simit.

Download MP4 All episodes

How it's made. Nothing here is a photo, a drawing file or a video model. A JavaScript function paints each frame from nothing: the domes and pencil minarets of the old city, Galata Tower, Kız Kulesi, the ferry with its flag, the gulls and the simit. The ney and kanun play in makam Hicaz; the ship's horn and the gulls are synthesized too.
